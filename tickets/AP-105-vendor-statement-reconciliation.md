# AP-105 · Reconcile against vendor statements

**Type:** Story · **Priority:** Medium · **Estimate:** M

## Context

Vendors send a monthly statement showing what they think we owe. AP checks it
against our ledger and investigates any difference.

## Story

As an AP clerk, I want to enter a vendor's statement balance and statement
date and see how it differs from our ledger, so that I can find and fix the
cause.

## Acceptance criteria

- The AP team can enter a statement balance and statement date for a vendor.
- The app shows the difference from our ledger and the entries that likely
  explain it.
- Tests cover the new behaviour.
